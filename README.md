A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for **Two Lines to Your Past Self** — two lines: a warning to your past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The entry

> When the doctor says 'come back in six months,' don't nod and walk out — ask him to say it again, slower.
> That second hearing is when you catch the word 'urgent,' and catching it is the only reason you're still here to write this.

230 characters. Two lines, one sentence each.

Why this moment: most warnings to a past self are about love or money, told in general terms. This one is a single appointment, a single quoted sentence, and a single physical act — the nod that almost lets it slide. The second line traces the cause and effect without restating the warning: ask again, hear "urgent", act on it, and you are still alive. And the last phrase closes the loop — the writer is only here to send the warning because the warning worked. That is the twist, and it sharpens the moment instead of decorating it: the entry itself is proof the advice was followed.

```sh
node main.ts
```
